---
title: "Creating a New OneLink Link"
ref: "onelink"
parent: "app-requirements"
---

1. First, create a new OneLink template in the AppsFlyer account dedicated to the app.
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/d5f9504e05b0.png' | relative_url }})

1. Go to OneLink Management and click Get started
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/c631c7e7bc3f.png' | relative_url }})

1. Select your app from the drop-down list and enter any subdomain
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/d56bb145cb37.png' | relative_url }})

1. Then create a new template
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/d623d36c5ae9.png' | relative_url }})

1. **Copy the resulting link and add it to the app card.**
1. Next, configure the link parameters
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/cf0d3f8448f9.png' | relative_url }})

1. Enable Retargeting
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/cc0a2be784ee.png' | relative_url }})

1. In the context menu, choose Edit link
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/dad4b7210e80.png' | relative_url }})

1. Go to Deep linking & redirection and fill in the deep link value and additional deep link value fields
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/f6a9c2d917ec.png' | relative_url }})

1. If everything is filled in correctly, the entered values appear on the right
   ![]({{ '/assets/img/c46a2f7ec3f7.png' | relative_url }})

1. Then click Update link

> To simulate a non-organic install, append the `is_retargeting=true` parameter to the OneLink link.
>
> Otherwise only the very first install will be recorded as non-organic.
{: .callout .note}
