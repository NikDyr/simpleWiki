> *Starting November 1st, 2025, all new apps and updates to existing apps submitted to Google Play and targeting Android 15+ devices must support 16 KB page sizes.*
{: .callout .info}

**To meet these requirements in Unity, use the following versions:**

- Unity 6000.1+ (released February 9, 2025)
- Unity 6000.0.38f1 (released February 14, 2025)
- Unity 2022.3.56f1 (released January 15, 2025)
- Unity 2021.3.48f1 (released January 22, 2025) — available under extended LTS support for Enterprise/Industry customers

[More details](https://discussions.unity.com/t/info-unity-engine-support-for-16-kb-memory-page-sizes-android-15/1589588)

Flutter and Kotlin builds must also be updated to support 16 KB page sizes.

[Support 16 KB page sizes (Android Developers)](https://developer.android.com/guide/practices/page-sizes#build)
